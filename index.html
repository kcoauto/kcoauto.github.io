K.Co Auto Post

A web dashboard under development for organizing TikTok video content, captions and publishing schedules.

Current local prototype features include video library management, caption preparation and a posting queue. TikTok account authorization and direct publishing are planned features and are not currently available.

How it works

Creators prepare videos and captions, organize a schedule and review their publishing queue. Future TikTok integrations will require user authorization and applicable platform approval.
